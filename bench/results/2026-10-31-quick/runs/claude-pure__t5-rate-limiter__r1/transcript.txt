**What's in place**

- **`src/limiter.js`** exports `{ createLimiter }`. The bucket starts full and reads `now()` once at creation. Refill adds `elapsed × refillPerSecond / 1000` and caps at capacity. A backwards clock adds nothing and moves the reference back to the earlier time. `msUntil` returns `ceil((n − tokens) × 1000 / refillPerSecond)`.
- **`test/limiter.test.js`** uses `node:test` and `node:assert` with an injected clock. It covers the starting state, `tryRemove` success and failure, default `n`, continuous and fractional refill, the capacity cap, `msUntil` rounding and its agreement with `tryRemove`, `reset`, the backwards clock, and every invalid input from the spec.

**Choices I made that the spec didn't settle**

- `msUntil(n)` with `n > capacity` throws `RangeError`, as the spec's "same rules" line implies.
- `reset()` also moves the time reference to `now()`.
- A `now` option that isn't a function is not validated separately. Calling it fails with a plain `TypeError`.
