## Decisions for the Developer

- Validate `text` first and throw `TypeError` when it is not a string.
- Use built-in Unicode decomposition and combining-mark removal for diacritics.
- Lowercase before filtering to `[a-z0-9]`.
- Replace every run outside `[a-z0-9]` with one `-`, then trim boundary separators.
- Apply `maxLength` only for a positive integer.
- Preserve whole hyphen-delimited words when possible.
- Hard-cut only when the first word itself exceeds `maxLength`.
- Ensure truncation never returns a trailing `-`.
- Do not add abstractions or dependencies.
