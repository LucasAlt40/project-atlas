**What's in `src/limiter.js`**
- `module.exports = { createLimiter }`, as specified.
- Validation throws `RangeError` for a bad `capacity` (not a positive integer) or `refillPerSecond` (not a positive finite number). `Number.isFinite` also rejects non-numbers such as `'1'`.
- `refill()` adds `elapsed * refillPerSecond / 1000` tokens, capped at `capacity`. If the clock goes backwards, it adds nothing and moves the reference time back to the new reading.
- `tryRemove`, `msUntil` and `available` all refill first. `msUntil` returns `Math.ceil` of the missing tokens divided by the refill rate, in milliseconds.
- `n` must be a positive integer, and `n > capacity` throws for both `tryRemove` and `msUntil`.
- `reset()` sets tokens back to capacity.

**Choices I made**
- The bucket reads `now()` once at creation to set its reference time. It does not wait for the first call.
- `reset()` does not touch the reference time. It only restores the token count.
- `createLimiter()` with no options throws the capacity `RangeError`. It does not throw a `TypeError`.
