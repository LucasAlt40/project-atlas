**What's in `src/limiter.js`**
- `createLimiter(options)` validates `capacity` (positive integer) and `refillPerSecond` (positive finite number) and throws `RangeError` otherwise. Missing options also throw `RangeError`.
- Tokens are a float that grows with elapsed time and is capped at `capacity`. `tryRemove`, `available`, `msUntil` and `reset` all refill first.
- `n` must be a positive integer no greater than `capacity`, or `tryRemove` and `msUntil` throw `RangeError`.
- If the clock goes backwards, no tokens are added or lost, and the earlier time becomes the new reference.
- `module.exports = { createLimiter }`, exactly as specified.

**Choices I made**
- The bucket's reference time is read from `now()` at creation, not on the first call.
- `reset()` refills first and then sets the bucket to full, so the reference time stays current.
- Float accumulation can in principle leave a count like 2.9999999 where 3 is expected. `available()` would then floor to 2. I did not add an epsilon to paper over that.
