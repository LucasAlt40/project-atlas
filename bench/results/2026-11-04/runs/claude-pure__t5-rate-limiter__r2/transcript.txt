The module exports `module.exports = { createLimiter }` and follows the spec:

- **Validation:** `capacity` must be a positive integer and `refillPerSecond` a positive finite number. Anything else throws `RangeError` from `createLimiter`.
- **Token count:** tokens are fractional and refill continuously, capped at `capacity`. The bucket starts full.
- **Methods:** `tryRemove`, `available`, `msUntil` and `reset` all refill first. Each `n` argument must be a positive integer no greater than `capacity`, or it throws `RangeError`.
- **Clock going backwards:** no tokens are added or lost, and the earlier time becomes the new reference point.

Choices I made that you may want to change:

- **Float tolerance:** comparisons use a 1e-9 epsilon so that a bucket which should hold exactly `n` tokens is not treated as one short because of rounding. `available()` and `msUntil()` apply the same tolerance.
- **Reference time:** the initial time is read from `now()` when `createLimiter` is called.
- **Option checks:** `now` is not validated. Passing a non-function will fail when the limiter is first used.

The tests use an injected clock and cover validation, refill timing, fractional refill, capping, `msUntil` rounding (including agreement with `tryRemove`), `reset`, and the clock-backwards rule.
