## Root causes and fixes in `src/cart.js`

| Customer report | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 3 × 19.90 shows 19.9 | `sum += item.price` ignored `qty` | `sum += item.price * item.qty` |
| TENOFF on 5.00 gives −5 | Fixed discount never clamped | `Math.max(0, sum)` after applying the coupon |
| OFF20 on 100 gives 20 | Percent branch returned the discount amount | `sum * (100 - value) / 100` |
| Long decimals like 0.30000000000000004 | No rounding | `Math.round(sum * 100) / 100` |
| Unknown coupons (and inherited names like `toString`) | `COUPONS[code]` found inherited `Object.prototype` members, so `toString` was treated as a coupon and produced NaN | `Object.hasOwn(COUPONS, couponCode)` check |

The export is unchanged (`module.exports = { total }`), and the JSDoc now states that the result is rounded to cents and never negative.
