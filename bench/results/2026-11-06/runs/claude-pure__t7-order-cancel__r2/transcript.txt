**Changes**
- **`src/inventory.js`**: added `restock(sku, qty)`, the counterpart of `take`.
- **`src/orders.js`**: added `cancel(orderId, reason)`.
  - The reason is checked first and must be a non-empty string after trimming, or it throws a `TypeError`.
  - A missing order throws `order N not found`. An already-cancelled order throws `order N is already cancelled`. Neither changes anything.
  - Otherwise it restocks the items, sets `status` to `'cancelled'`, stores the reason on the order as `reason`, emits `order.cancelled` with `{ orderId, reason }`, and returns a copy of the order.
- **`src/reports.js`**: `revenue()` and `unitsSold(sku)` now skip cancelled orders.
- **`test/cancel.test.js`** (new): covers stock restoration, the event, the status and reason, not-found, double cancel, invalid reasons, and report exclusion.

**Choices you may want to change**

- Whitespace-only reasons are rejected as well as the empty string.
- The cancelled reason is kept as a plain `reason` field on the order record.
