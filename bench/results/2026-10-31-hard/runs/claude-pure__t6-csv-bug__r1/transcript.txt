**Root causes in the old code**

- It split every line on `,` and every text on `\n`, so quoted commas and line breaks broke rows and fields.
- It removed quotes only when a field both started and ended with one. It never unescaped `""`.
- `text.split('\n')` produced an empty last row for a trailing newline, and it left `\r` on CRLF lines.
- It had no error for an unterminated quote.

**The fix**

`parseCsv` is now a character-level state machine. The exports are unchanged.

- A quote opens a quoted field only at the start of a field. Inside quotes, `""` becomes `"`, and a single `"` closes the field.
- Commas end fields. `\n` ends a row. A `\r` directly before `\n` is skipped.
- A newline inside quotes is kept as field content.
- A trailing newline adds no row, and empty input returns `[]`.
- An unclosed quote throws `SyntaxError`.

**Tests added to `test/csv.test.js`**

Ten new cases cover each reported bug, plus empty fields, an empty quoted field, empty input and the unterminated-quote error. I traced the key cases by hand against the new code, including the `"say ""hi"""` case, the CRLF case, and the embedded-newline case, and they should pass.

**Choices I made beyond the bug list**

- A quote that appears in the middle of an unquoted field, such as `ab"c`, is kept as a literal character. RFC 4180 calls that malformed, and I chose not to throw on it.
- Text after a closing quote, such as `"a"b`, is appended to the field rather than rejected.
- A bare `\r` that is not followed by `\n` is kept as content.
